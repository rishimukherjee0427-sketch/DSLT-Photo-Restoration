# Claims vs evidence audit of `paper/latex/main.tex`

Audited: working copy of `paper/latex/main.tex` (uncommitted edits included, 266 lines), `paper/build/out/numbers.tex`, the eight generated tables in `paper/build/out/tables/`, and the figure captions. Numbers were re-derived from the raw per-image JSON files, manifests, histories and git history (not from the macros). Date of audit: 2026-11-25. Nothing was edited or run on the GPU; one CPU-only model construction was used.

Version note: line numbers and quotes refer to the 266-line working copy read at the start of the audit. While the audit ran, commit 869ad25 (16:09:57) changed `main.tex` (267 lines): Restormer was removed from the Baselines paragraph, the baseline `\pending` and Limitations gained a new item (6) "Baselines: only U-Net and NAFNet were trained; Restormer not trained for compute reasons", and the real-photo figure/text moved into Limitation (4). Rows 24, 35, 36 and 73 therefore still apply to U-Net and NAFNet (no results exist yet; claim still NOT SUPPORTED until `results/v10_baselines/` is populated), but the Restormer-specific parts are now moot. Line numbers after the Models section shift by about +1.

Path shorthand: `OUT` = `paper/build/out`; `S0` = `results/v7_stage0` (A/B/C seeds 123 and 2026 come from `results/v7_corrected`); `S2` = `results/v8_translator_training`; `S3` = `results/v9_convergence_pilot`. Status key: SUPPORTED / PARTLY / NOT SUPPORTED / UNVERIFIABLE (cannot be checked from the repository) / PENDING (red `\pending` marker in the text, or the supporting result does not exist yet).

Context facts found while auditing (used in several rows):
- Stage 2 (800 steps) and Stage 3 (20k steps) differ in more than the step count: crop 128 vs 256, EMA 0.99 vs 0.999, warm-up 50 vs 500, validation set 24 vs 250 images per severity (manifests, `args`). The paper's "budget" effect is a joint effect of all of these.
- The 44.4M "full model" count is enc+dec+translators at inference. It excludes the 1.56M damage estimator (training-only auxiliary head in ungated configs, `S2/architecture_verification.json`). Gated variants are 46.06M (`S0/profiling_table.md`).
- The divergence diagnostic (`S3/diag/`, untracked) is still running (a process `diag_divergence.py --steps 5000` was alive during the audit; `steps.jsonl` had ~1,530 lines; `diag.log` is empty). It covers only the first ~1,500 steps of seed 2026, not the 20k-step run.
- Baseline training (`experiments/run_baselines.sh`, untracked) was waiting/just starting. `results/v10_baselines/` contains only an empty `timing/` directory. The script schedules U-Net and NAFNet only (seed 42); Restormer is not scheduled.

### (2) Prioritised overreach / unsupported claims and suggested fixes

Priority 1: claims that are contradicted by, or have no support in, the stored results (fix before submission).

1. **Rows 62 and 61, L174-175, "gradient clipping was active throughout" / "no step produced non-finite values".** Diagnostic data show pre-clip norms above 1.0 on only 43% of steps 1-1,510, with median 0.90, and norms of 1e6-4e7 from step ~1,490; no gradient data exist beyond ~1,530 steps. Drop the clipping sentence or quote the measured fractions after the diagnostic finishes.

Revise Limitation (1) accordingly (keep "still improving", drop "lead still widening").

Observations about macro semantics (not numeric mismatches):
- `nOCSevPSNR` is the severe O-C value (+0.017), not the maximum over severities (medium is +0.028). The text names "on severe", so it is consistent.
- `nGatedLPIPScount` counts seed-averaged point estimates, not significant comparisons (7 of 9 are Holm-significant).
- `nMemCut` and `nStepCut` average over all three Stage 3 runs per model, including the diverged Tfull run (run-to-run spread is small: 7.19-7.20 GB; 0.826-0.841 s/step).
- The C-A severe LPIPS Holm p-value differs by family (0.042 in `abc_3seed_family`, 0.061 in `oracle_family`); neither is quoted in the paper.
- Tables `tab_gating_stats.tex` and `tab_translator_stats.tex` are generated but not `\input`; the text cites CIs and verdicts from them.
